# RePrompt - Reverse-Engineering AI Prompts

> RePrompt reverse-engineers visual media into structured AI generation prompts and artifact-suppression negative prompts for Midjourney v7, FLUX 1.1 Pro, Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large, DALL-E 3, and Google Gemini.

## Overview
- **Application Name**: RePrompt
- **Description**: Upload an image or video clip to deconstruct composition, estimated lens look, depth of field, lighting scheme, textures, and subject details into high-fidelity AI generation prompts.
- **Privacy Standard**: Zero Image Storage Architecture. Images and video frames are held transiently in RAM only for the duration of the analysis request and are never written to disk or stored.

## Generation Engine Modes (Image)
1. **Universal**: Portable, generator-agnostic prompt for all modern models.
2. **Midjourney v7**: Formatted with aesthetic descriptors, parameters (`--ar`, `--v 7`), and `--style raw`.
3. **FLUX 1.1 Pro**: Natural language descriptive art direction with optical characteristics.
4. **Stable Diffusion 3.5 Large**: Structured descriptive clauses with dedicated negative prompts.
5. **DALL-E 3**: Coherent descriptive prose detailing spatial composition and ambiance.
6. **Gemini / Imagen 3**: Natural-language visual instructions with clean spatial grounding.

## Detail Level Control
- **Simple**: 1-2 concise sentences focusing on core subject and main lighting.
- **Detailed**: Balanced 3-4 sentences covering subject, composition, environment, and textures.
- **Professional**: Technical visual direction with estimated lens look, lighting setup, and material finishes.
